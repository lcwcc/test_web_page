  <p class="eyebrow">About this journal</p>
  <h1>A map for reading, not a substitute for care.</h1>

  Metabolic Evidence Fieldnotes is an independent educational project about research interpretation. It focuses on the questions that frequently disappear when complex peptide and incretin studies are compressed into short claims.

  Each fieldnote distinguishes mechanism from outcome, association from causation, and research context from personal medical decisions. References to external archives are provided as paths for further reading, not endorsements of products or treatment.

  The journal does not provide medical advice. Readers should consult qualified clinicians and current regulatory information for decisions about diagnosis, medication, dosing, contraindications, or adverse effects.
